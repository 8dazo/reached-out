# Funding Signal Outreach Lane

## Purpose

Use recent funding as an early hiring/growth signal alongside normal job discovery. This lane complements YC Work at a Startup, HiringCafe, Wellfound, and official career pages; it does not replace role-based outreach.

## Sources

Check current funding announcements from official company posts first, then reputable coverage such as TechCrunch, Crunchbase News, Sifted, VentureBeat, Reuters, Business Wire, and PR Newswire. Also inspect Y Combinator company pages and recent YC batches for companies with active engineering hiring or fresh growth signals.

## Timing

- Hot: funding announced in the last 14 days.
- Acceptable fallback: up to 30 days when the company is an unusually strong technical fit or there is separate evidence of team growth/hiring.
- Prefer pre-seed, seed, Series A, and Series B because founder/technical-leader outreach is most actionable at these stages.
- Series C+ only gets a strong boost when there is an explicit engineering hiring or expansion signal.

## Eligibility

The company must be headquartered in one of the target regions: US, Europe/UK/Switzerland/Norway, or Singapore. Companies headquartered in India remain excluded. The contact is selected by hiring relevance, never by inferred nationality or ethnicity.

For a company with a live role, normal role eligibility rules still apply: Devansh must have a realistic India-remote, global/APAC remote, sponsorship, relocation, EOR, international-candidate, or overseas-contractor path.

For a newly funded company with no public role, proactive founder/technical-leader outreach is allowed only when all of these are true:
1. The funding event is fresh and confirmed by a reliable source.
2. The company is a strong AI/software/product engineering fit for Devansh.
3. An exact founder/CTO/technical-leader address is explicit public current or independently mailbox verified.

## Contact priority

For newly funded teams:
- 1–50 employees: technical founder → founder → CTO → Head of Engineering.
- 51–200 employees: Head of Engineering → hiring manager → CTO → technical recruiter.
- Later-stage/larger companies: only outreach when there is an actual relevant opening or clear team-growth signal.

Default to one contact per company.

## Messaging

If a relevant role exists, use the normal role-specific email and mention the funding only when it improves the opening naturally.

If there is no public role, use a short founder-style note:
- congratulate them on the confirmed round;
- mention one concrete product/technical reason Devansh is relevant;
- give one or two proof points only;
- ask whether they are building the engineering/AI team or if there is someone appropriate to speak with;
- never state or imply they are hiring unless a source confirms it;
- no resume attachment by default.

## Scoring boost

Add a meaningful score boost for funding age and round, but never allow funding alone to override poor technical fit, impossible geography, weak contact evidence, seniority mismatch, or an invalid email.

Track reply outcomes by funding source, funding round, days since announcement, contact type, and whether a live role existed. Use qualified replies/interviews—not raw sends—as the optimization target.
